↩ Returns & Refund Policy

At Poshak Story, we want you to be completely happy with your purchase. If for any reason you are not satisfied, we offer hassle-free returns within 7 days of delivery.

1
Initiate Return

Go to My Orders, find your order, and click Request Return. Select the reason for return. Returns must be initiated within 7 days of delivery.

2
Pack the Item

Pack the item securely in its original packaging with all tags intact. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with all accessories.

3
Schedule Pickup or Drop-off

Our courier partner will schedule a free pickup from your address. Alternatively, you can drop off the item at our store: Shop 7, Sector 17, Chandigarh.

4
Refund Processed

Once we receive and inspect the item, a full refund will be credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. COD orders are refunded to your bank account.

❌ Non-Returnable Items

  • Items worn, washed, or damaged by the customer
  • Items without original tags or packaging
  • Customised or altered garments
  • Items purchased during final clearance sales
